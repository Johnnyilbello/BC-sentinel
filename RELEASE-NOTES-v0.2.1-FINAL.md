# BC Sentinel v0.2.1 Final — UX & Motion Polish

Scanning now gives immediate feedback at the click rather than waiting for the first worker progress event.

- independent 100 ms elapsed-time clock (`mm:ss.t`);
- scan buttons disable immediately;
- indeterminate preparation state before real file progress arrives;
- smooth 135 ms interpolation between authoritative progress values;
- premium activity ring, refined page fades and micro-fades;
- clearer cancel/completion states;
- motion remains presentation-only and respects the Animazioni setting;
- Dashboard Gestisci now correctly opens Protezione.

ETW, Telemetry Service, taskbar/tray icons, scanner decisions, scoring and quarantine are unchanged.
